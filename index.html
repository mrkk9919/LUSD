Transparency

Reserves You Can
Actually Check

LUSD is designed around a full-reserve model. Every unit in circulation is meant to be matched by an equivalent amount of USDC held in reserve, with the reserve address published so that anyone can verify the balance on-chain.

  • ✓Reserve address published and readable on a public block explorer.
  • ✓Mint and burn events emitted on-chain and permanently traceable.
  • ✓Independent audit report to be published before any mainnet launch.
  • ✓Contract source code open for public review.

Risk Disclosures

What You Should Understand First

Value & Redemption

  • The 1:1 relationship is a design target, not a guarantee of market price or of redemption.
  • Market price on secondary venues can deviate from $1.00 if liquidity is thin.
  • Redemption depends on the reserve being available and the platform remaining operational.
  • Reserve backing, audits and redemption arrangements have not been independently verified.

Operational & Regulatory

  • Smart contracts can contain bugs. Funds sent to an incorrect address cannot be recovered.
  • Accounts may be frozen where required by law, sanctions screening or platform terms.
  • Stablecoin rules differ by country and are still changing. Access may be restricted.
  • Nothing on this page is legal, tax or investment advice.
FAQ

Common Questions

Is 1 LUSD always worth exactly $1?
$1.00 is the design target. LUSD is built to be minted and redeemed 1:1 against USDC, which is itself a dollar-referenced asset. On secondary markets the traded price can move slightly with liquidity, so the target should not be read as a guarantee.
How is LUSD backed?
By a full-reserve model: USDC received on deposit is held in reserve, and LUSD is issued 1:1 against it. Burning LUSD releases the corresponding USDC. The reserve address is published so the balance can be checked on-chain.
Can I redeem at any time?
Redemption is designed to be open at any time, with no lock-up and no exit penalty. It can be paused in exceptional circumstances — for example a contract upgrade, a security incident, or a legal requirement.
Which networks does LUSD support?
The current build targets Base. Additional networks would be deployed as separate contracts, each with its own reserve accounting, rather than as a single cross-chain balance.
Are there fees?
Minting and redemption are designed to be 1:1 with no protocol fee. Network gas fees still apply and are paid to the chain, not to the issuer. Any future fee would be published here before it takes effect.
Why would an account be frozen?
Freezing is a compliance control, not a commercial one. It applies where there is a sanctions match, a court or regulator order, or clear evidence of fraud. A frozen account can still receive funds, but cannot transfer or redeem until the matter is resolved.

Important Notice

The 1:1 USD relationship is a design target, not a guarantee of market price or redemption. Reserve backing, audits and redemption arrangements have not been independently verified.

LUSD is a digital asset concept. Its actual value, availability and dependability depend on market conditions, platform support, regulatory requirements and other factors. Please understand the risks before using it.
