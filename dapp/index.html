About

LUSDT is designed around the goal of maintaining a value reference to the US dollar. Its intended 1:1 relationship provides a simple framework for understanding its target value in digital financial environments.

Actual market prices may fluctuate, and the target value should not be interpreted as a guarantee of redemption or market price stability.

USD ReferenceDesigned with a target value of 1 LUSDT = 1 USD.
Digital TransferIntended for digital value transfers on supported blockchain networks, subject to actual platform capabilities.
